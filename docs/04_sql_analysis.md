# SQL analysis. Conclusions

## Exploratory Analysis
- About **100 000** orders in database
- A total GMV(Gross Merchandise Value) is about 16 million
- Average delivery time is about 12,56 days
- 91 % of orders were deliverede on time
- Average Order Value is about **154,1**
- 3,12 % of unique customers have placed more than one order
- Cancellation rate is relative small (less than 1% percent but does not take into account **unavailable**)
- SP (Sao-Paulo) provides the highest revenue

## Deep Analysis
- Customer retention is the main opportunity
- Only a small fraction of customers make repeat purchases
- Trade is highly concentrated in Sao Paulo
- Revenue is generated by a small number of high-demand categories (health beauty, toys, pet shop)
- Customers that buy more than once generate more revenue in average than those who make only one order
- One-time customers spend not much more money on order than repeat customers (161,82 vs 148,85)
- Delivery is rather slow (the lowest average delivery time by categories is about 6 days)
- In some states problems with delivery even worse
- That may be the reason why trade is highly concentrated in Sao Paulo
